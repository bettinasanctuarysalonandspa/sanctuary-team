# Sanctuary team

Build a private, professional AI-powered application called Sanctuary Salon AI for Sanctuary Salon & Spa in Cornelius, North Carolina.

The application will be a centralized employee resource that combines four tools:

Consultation Calculator: Accurate stylist-level service pricing, extension pricing, home-care recommendations, retail totals, applicable taxes, amenity charges, and client consultation guidance.

Sanctuary Emergency Help: Step-by-step assistance with client concerns, salon policies, difficult situations, and escalation procedures.

Sanctuary Salon AI: Employee education, standard operating procedures, service knowledge, hospitality standards, and our Code of Honor.

Marketing & Content Studio: Social media captions, video scripts, marketing hooks, and client education.

Create an elegant, modern, mobile-first experience that is intuitive for hairstylists and easy to navigate.

The design should feel like a luxury salon and wellness brand: sophisticated, clean, welcoming, and professional.

Include a beautiful home dashboard with four clearly labeled tools, employee login capabilities, and a separate administrator area where the owner can manage information.

IMPORTANT: Do not invent salon policies, prices, employee levels, or product information. We will supply the approved information and build each tool step by step.

Begin by creating the application structure, navigation, and visual design. Do not build the full AI functionality or invent database records yet.
